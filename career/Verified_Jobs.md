## Avoid / likely spam

- **Viraaj HR Solutions** "NodeJS Developer" posts (₹10–30 LPA, many cities, no real job description). This is a mass-posting agency pattern, so don't send documents.
- Any job asking for a **fee, deposit, laptop purchase, or paid "certification"** before joining. Genuine employers never charge.
- Recruiters who move to **WhatsApp or Telegram only** and won't share a company email or website.
- Naukri listings marked "3+ weeks ago" whose job ID ends in an old date (for example `…-040923…` means 2023). These are stale reposts.

## How to check any job yourself in 1 minute

1. Open the company's own website and look for the same role on their **Careers** page.
2. Check that the recruiter's email uses the **company domain**, not gmail.com.
3. Look up the company on LinkedIn: real employee profiles, posts, and an office address.
4. Search "<company name> reviews" on AmbitionBox or Glassdoor.
